# Consent to take part: open-ended computer work study

_Template. Replace with the IRB-approved text before recruiting anyone outside the research group._

**Who is running this.** Keru Wang (kw2727@nyu.edu) and collaborators at NYU.

**What the study is about.** How people's goals take shape while they do open-ended work on a computer, and whether an AI model can follow that from a record of the work.

**What you will do.** One session of about one hour on your own computer: a task of your choice within a family we assign, while thinking aloud. A chime every 10 minutes asks you to say in one sentence what you are trying to do. Afterwards you answer a few typed questions, review what was recorded, and upload the recording.

**What is recorded.**
- Screenshots of all your monitors, and the text on screen, whenever something changes and at least every 15 seconds.
- Clicks (with the clicked element's name), app and window switches, scrolling, and **the text you type**.
- Your microphone for the whole session.
- Not recorded: windows of password managers; private or incognito browser windows. Anything you delete in the review step.

**What we do with it.** The recording is stored on NYU-controlled storage, accessible to the research team only. We transcribe the audio, derive text histories of the session, and show those histories to AI models. We may quote short, de-identified excerpts in publications. We will not publish screenshots or audio without separate, explicit permission.

**Risks.** The recording may contain personal information that appears on your screen. You control what is uploaded: you can delete any app or time range before upload, and you can ask us to delete your session at any time by emailing the contact above.

**Voluntary.** You can stop at any time (Ctrl+C in the terminal) and choose not to upload. There is no penalty.

**Compensation.** _To be filled in._

By typing "yes" in the program you confirm that you have read this and agree to take part.
